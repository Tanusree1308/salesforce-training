# Day 1: Salesforce CRM Basics

## 1. What is CRM?
**CRM (Customer Relationship Management)** is a centralized platform used to manage all company-customer interactions. It replaces fragmented tools like spreadsheets with a single **"Customer 360"** view.

* **The Goal:** To reduce technical complexity and increase productivity.
* **The Impact:** 89% of customers see a positive ROI within their first year.

---

## 2. Platform Interface & Navigation
The Lightning Experience interface is designed for high productivity:

* **Home:** A customizable dashboard showing daily tasks and performance.
* **Global Search:** Use the `/` hotkey to find any record instantly.
* **App Launcher:** The "Waffle Icon" used to switch between apps.
* **Setup:** The "Cog Wheel" for backend configurations.

---

## 3. Core Objects (The Building Blocks)
Salesforce data is structured into **Objects**:

* **Leads:** Unqualified potential customers.
* **Accounts:** The companies you do business with.
* **Contacts:** The individuals associated with those Accounts.
* **Opportunities:** Qualified business deals in progress.

---

## 4. The Sales Lifecycle
1. **Nurturing:** Tracking interactions to see if they meet criteria.
2. **Conversion:** A Lead is converted into an **Account**, **Contact**, and **Opportunity**.

---

## 5. Automation & Tools
* **Kanban View:** A visual board to track deal progression.
* **Flow Builder:** An automation tool used to create workflows without code.
* **Object Manager:** Where admins manage data architecture and fields.
